# Local English public-domain Bible texts

Sources: official eBible.org VPL archives, pinned by SHA-256 in metadata.json.
Included editions: WEB Classic, ASV 1901, YLT 1898, Webster 1833, Darby 1884, Geneva 1599, BSB, ERV 1885, Douay-Rheims 1899, WEBBE, and MSB. DRA and WEBBE include the seven deuterocanonical books; DRA also includes the longer Esther and Daniel chapter divisions.
The VPL format contains Bible text only. The importer preserves verse wording; 66-book editions include the standard canon, while DRA and WEBBE include the seven deuterocanonical books. Empty verse markers and verse-number gaps are preserved, never filled from another edition.
The World English Bible name is retained only for the unchanged source text. The Majority Standard Bible is identified as Public Domain by its official eBible.org source.
