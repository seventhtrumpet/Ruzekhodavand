Source: janvier-s/original-douay-rheims, pinned commit 5bcc57fa20aae5acdc494c13b047b6287fec0b29.

Both modernized and original spelling source JSON files are copied without changing their text. The reader displays verse text only; annotations, notes, and front matter are not rendered as scripture.
The 76 listed texts comprise the 73-book Catholic canon plus 3 appendix texts.
